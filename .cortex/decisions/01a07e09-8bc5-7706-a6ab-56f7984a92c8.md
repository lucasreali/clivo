The panels wrapping the client list (CustomerList) and the day list (DayPanel) carried `overflow-hidden` to keep a row's hover background inside the panel's 8px corner radius. That also clipped the row's `⋯` menu (src/shared/ui/Menu.tsx), which is a `<details>` popup positioned `absolute top-full right-0`: on the last row it was cut off at the panel's bottom edge.

They now carry `overflow-x-clip` instead. `overflow-x: clip` with `overflow-y: visible` is the one pairing CSS keeps asymmetric — a `visible` axis next to `hidden` computes to `auto`, but next to `clip` it stays `visible`. The corner radius still clips the hover background (verified in the browser on the last row), and the menu opens past the panel.

Reach for the same pairing on any new list panel that hosts a popup; do not go back to `overflow-hidden` there. A popup that must escape a scrolling ancestor, rather than a merely clipping one, would need a portal instead — no list needs that yet.
